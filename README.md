# Amazon Sales Dashboard

Excel dashboard analyzing 10,000 Amazon sales transactions (2013–2014, India) 
to track revenue trends, category performance, and regional patterns.

## Overview
Built pivot tables and interactive charts from raw transactional sales data 
to uncover revenue drivers by product category, region, and time period — 
supporting data-driven inventory and marketing decisions.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Formulas)

## Key Insights
- Analyzed ₹1.02 crore in total revenue across 33,874 units sold over 2 years
- Identified a major seasonal spike: November–December sales were ~13x 
  a typical month, indicating strong holiday-driven demand
- North region generated 36% of total revenue, the highest of all regions
- Top-performing category (Nestle Smarties Pop-Up) drove ₹15.9L in revenue, 
  ahead of all other product lines
- Maharashtra and Delhi were the top revenue-generating states
